# Texture licenses

Originals are not in the repo. Files are built by `scripts/make-textures.mjs <source-dir>` (a source folder that is missing is skipped), except `metal.webp` (`scripts/make-metal.mjs`).
`paper.webp`, `tape.webp` and `tape-masking.webp` are real scans kept as grayscale photos; `textures.css` multiplies them onto a palette colour (`background-blend-mode`). The other tiles are ALPHA-ONLY (white RGB, alpha = intensity, 6-16 alpha levels so lossless WebP alpha stays small), painted in palette colours through `mask-image`.
All ambientCG assets: author ambientCG (Lennart Demes), license CC0 1.0 (https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/), downloaded 2026-11-12 (1K-JPG).

## paper.webp
- Source: Paper001, https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Paper001 (https://ambientcg.com/get?file=Paper001_1K-JPG.zip, `*_Color.jpg`)
- Author: ambientCG (Lennart Demes); License: CC0 1.0, https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/; Date: 2026-11-12
- Processing: grayscale, a 600px square of the 1024x600 scan downscaled to 512px, slow shading removed (blur radius 20), made seamless by cross-fade blend, levels set around near-white (mean 236). Opaque lossy WebP (q 0.5), drawn at 256px on polaroid frames and Card Stock.

## metal.webp
- Source: procedural, generated by `scripts/make-metal.mjs` (own work, no third-party source); Date: 2026-11-12
- Processing: per-row random streak values softened vertically and varied slowly along x, plus 90 long fine scratches with wrap-around; seamless 512x256px, 16 alpha levels. Alpha WebP.

## tape.webp, tape-2.webp, tape-3.webp
- Source: Tape005, https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Tape005 (https://ambientcg.com/get?file=Tape005_1K-JPG.zip, `*_Color.jpg` and `*_Opacity.jpg`)
- Author: ambientCG (Lennart Demes); License: CC0 1.0, https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/; Date: 2026-11-13
- Processing: three different strips (the whole clean bottom-middle strip, the top 300px of one long strip, the bottom 300px of another; none has a wrinkle or scratch), cut out with the scan's own opacity map, rotated 90 degrees to lie flat (about 295x142), grayscale with contrast raised around light gray. Lossy WebP (q 0.7) with alpha = the strip's outline. Replaces the earlier Tape006 crepe strip.

## tape-masking.webp
- Source: Tape005, https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Tape005 (https://ambientcg.com/get?file=Tape005_1K-JPG.zip, `*_Color.jpg` and `*_Opacity.jpg`)
- Author: ambientCG (Lennart Demes); License: CC0 1.0, https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/; Date: 2026-11-13
- Processing: the whole clean bottom-middle strip (smooth beige masking tape, no wrinkle or scratch), cut out with the scan's own opacity map, rotated 90 degrees to lie flat (295x142), grayscale with contrast raised around light gray. Lossy WebP (q 0.7) with alpha = the strip's outline. Used by the "Masking" tape kind.

## print-wear.webp
- Source: SurfaceImperfections017, https://ambientcg.com/view?id=SurfaceImperfections017 (https://ambientcg.com/get?file=SurfaceImperfections017_1K-JPG.zip)
- Author: ambientCG (Lennart Demes); License: CC0 1.0, https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/; Date: 2026-11-12
- Processing: grayscale, downscaled 1024 to 512px, levelled/thresholded (brightest ~7% of pixels, smoothstep) into a sparse speckle tile: alpha = wear speck strength (16 levels), used as the mask of a surface-coloured wear overlay. The source is already tileable, so no cross-fade was applied. Alpha WebP.

## film-grain.webp
- Generated by scripts/make-textures.mjs, CC0. Seeded fine monochrome near-gaussian grain, 256px, tile-periodic by construction (wrap-around blur); the dark half of the grain becomes alpha (16 levels). Alpha WebP.

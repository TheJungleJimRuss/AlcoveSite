[First-Timer FAQ: Common Worries Answered]

[Demystifying the studio experience for musicians recording for the very first time.]

[Do I have to be completely rehearsed before coming in?]

[Not at all. Rough demos and voice memos are completely welcome. We can spend time structuring, dialing tempos, and rehearsing in the room together.]

[Can my friends or parents hang out during the session?]

[Absolutely. Alcove has a comfortable lounge couch where friends, bandmates, or parents can chill, listen, and support you.]

[What should I bring on tracking day?]

[Just bring your instrument (if you want to use yours), your lyrics/notes on your phone, and your ideas. We provide amps, mics, pedals, cables, and coffee/water.]

[How long does it take to get the final mix?]

[Typically within a few days of tracking. You will receive draft revisions to listen on your headphones and in the car before finalizing your master.]

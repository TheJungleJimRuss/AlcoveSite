About Alcove

Hi, I'm Seth. I make records, and I'd love to help you make yours.

Hey, I'm Seth

I produce, record, and mix music, and I started Alcove because recording studios can feel like closed doors. I wanted a place where young musicians could walk in, feel comfortable, and still leave with something they're proud of.

I care more about how a song feels than how perfect it is. If you're a teen artist or you've never been in a studio before, you're exactly who I built this for.

What I believe

Making music should be fun. If you're nervous, that's okay. We'll figure it out together.

The Space

A relaxed, open room where you can take a breath, get comfortable, and get creative.

The Tracking Area

This is where the playing happens. Plenty of space, nothing intimidating, and room to move around.

The Chill Lounge

A spot for bandmates, friends, or parents to hang out, listen along, and cheer you on.

The Creative Desk

Where we listen back together, tweak things, and shape the final mix side by side.

First-Timer Questions

Everything people usually wonder about before their first session.

Do I need to be totally rehearsed?

Not at all. Rough demos and voice memos are welcome. We can work out the structure and tempo together in the room.

Can my friends or parents come along?

Absolutely. There's room for bandmates, friends, or parents to hang out, listen, and cheer you on.

What should I bring?

Your instrument if you'd like to use your own, your lyrics or notes, and your ideas. If you're not sure what else you need, ask me and we'll sort it out before the session.

How long until I hear the finished song?

We'll talk timing before we start. You'll get to hear drafts and tell me what you'd change before anything's final.

Still Have Questions?

Want to say hi, ask something, or come see the room? Reach out anytime.
